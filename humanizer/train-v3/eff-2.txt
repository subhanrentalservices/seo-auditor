=== P3 news-blog register with hedges and asides
Turning off GPS on your phone, or using a camera that never had it, may not be quite the privacy fix people think it is. Many point and shoot cameras and SLRs that don't geotag still record the camera's serial number in the EXIF data, and that number can be used to search for other photos taken with the same camera. In one case in Australia, a man was able to find his stolen camera through stolencamerafinder.com after the thief uploaded a photo taken with it to Flickr (the thief, as it happened, had also put his address on the page). Had the address not been there, police could in principle have subpoenaed Flickr for the uploader's IP address, session times and logs, as they have reportedly tried to do with Twitter, and worked out where the upload came from. Put together, that sort of information can say a fair amount about where someone has been and, as the "w0rmer" case suggests, who they are.
